# Claim 6 limitations and deviations

- The run verifies the paper's core derivative-transfer identity on smooth
  Gaussian coupling factors rather than mechanically formalizing the full
  reciprocal-characteristic proof.
- Tensor-wise integration by parts reduces to the checked scalar identity;
  all index-triplet counts are recorded, but no wall-clock complexity claim is
  inferred from them.
- The dimension exponents come from exact source factors, not timing fits.
- Gaussian tails make boundary terms vanish; nonsmooth or heavy-tailed
  couplings outside the theorem assumptions are not tested.
