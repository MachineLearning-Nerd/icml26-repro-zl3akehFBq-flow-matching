# Claim 3 limitations and deviations

- “Faster” is primarily evaluated by observed minimum exact-KL resources for
  one assumption-satisfying bridge family; this finite family does not by
  itself prove the universal theorem.
- Integer second-phase lengths approximate requested `delta`; achieved
  endpoints and relative errors are recorded.
- Formula-selected work is secondary context only. The primary first-hit
  budgets are selected from achieved exact KL.
- Exact KL diagnostics use the solvable Claim 2 witness.
- The symbolic certificate covers the schedule/rate consequence, not a
  proof-assistant formalization of every stochastic estimate.
