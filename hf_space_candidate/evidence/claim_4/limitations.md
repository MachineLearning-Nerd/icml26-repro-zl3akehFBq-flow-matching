# Claim 4 limitations and deviations

- Strongly log-concave Gaussian couplings are a valid special case of weak
  log-concavity; nonconvex weakly log-concave examples are not evaluated.
- The actual Gaussian discretization error can decay faster than the theorem's
  upper-order allowance; no tightness claim is made.
- Hidden constants behind the comparison symbol are not estimated.
- Exact arithmetic formulas remove sampling uncertainty but do not cover every
  coupling satisfying H3, H6, and H7.
