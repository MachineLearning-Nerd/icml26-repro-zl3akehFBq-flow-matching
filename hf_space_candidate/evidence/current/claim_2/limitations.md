# Claim 2 limitations and deviations

- The target is a point mass. This is deliberate: Theorem 2 permits it through
  H2, while it creates a strict H4-without-H3 witness.
- Only partition-aligned early-stopping times are evaluated.
- The test is an exact solvable specialization, not a numerical proof for
  every distribution satisfying H1, H2, and H4.
- The experiment does not approach `delta=0`, which is outside Theorem 2.
