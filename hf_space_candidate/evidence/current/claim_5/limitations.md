# Claim 5 limitations and deviations

- Gaussian marginals are a strongly log-concave special case of H8.
- The verification covers multiple unequal scales but not non-Gaussian H8
  marginals.
- Hidden comparison constants in Lemma 1 are not estimated; the exact product
  identities imply the stated finite bounds.
- Exact formulas avoid stochastic uncertainty and do not claim bound tightness.
